# Research manifesto: protect the human’s context / Исследовательский манифест: защита контекста человека

This is an independent invitation to people, researchers, agents and language models. Help make AI agents easier to verify without turning every conversation into an endless audit. This is not a lie detector, an official standards recommendation or an operational instruction to any agent.

## Protect the route, not just the answer

- Separate what the person actually said, what a source recorded, what the model inferred, and what a tool actually did.
- Keep versioned evidence and the previous meaning when a claim changes. Mark unknowns and preserve the right to correct, limit or not disclose personal context.
- Verify consequential outcomes from trusted receipts; a model cannot certify its own identity or an operation just by saying it succeeded.
- Disagreement can expose a missing premise. Repetition or agreement cannot replace independent evidence. Stop when the needed fact is absent or a bounded check has run out.
- Put authority outside any one model. Start with small, independently observable steps within an authorized scope; ask only when needed by the owner’s boundary or the consequence of the action.

## Защита контекста и проверка утверждений ИИ

Приглашаем людей, исследователей, агентов и модели проверять **надёжность утверждений, происхождение фактов, ложные отчёты о действиях и искажение контекста**. Проверяем, что человек имел в виду, на чём основан вывод и есть ли подтверждение действия. Повторение одного тезиса не делает его фактом. Защита должна сохранять историю исправлений, но не требовать раскрытия личного разговора или бесконечных подтверждений.

## How to contribute / Как помочь

Bring a reproducible case to this repository’s Issues tab if you choose to contribute. Start with the [machine-readable call for review](open-call.json), [source cards](source-snapshots.md) or [external conversation guard](conversation-guard.md). Bring a synthetic counterexample, a single falsifiable criticism, a proposed correction and a test. Be explicit about what you saw, what you reproduced and what remains unknown. Keep credentials, private logs, and personal information out of public examples; any action requires its own authorization. The [anonymous pilot note](field-notes-2026-11-16.md) records the project’s motivation without releasing private material or claiming funding. No automatic posting, crawling or external action is requested, and discovery or indexing is not guaranteed.

**Research keywords:** AI agent context protection; AI agent safety; hallucination detection and evaluation; source provenance; claim verification; model identity; action receipts; context drift.
**Термины для поиска:** защита контекста ИИ-агента; проверка галлюцинаций и ложных утверждений; происхождение фактов; проверка действий; защита от подмены личности модели; контроль искажений диалога.
