# Anonymous field notes: hypotheses to test, not a benchmark

These are qualitative observations from the project's founder pilot. They are distinct from the fixed competition package and its anonymous evaluation. There is no controlled sample or estimated error rate. Names, product and programme titles, exact conversations and other identifying detail are not included.

| Observation in the pilot | Proposed check |
|---|---|
| In a short voice session, an assistant repeatedly used an inconsistent self-label after being corrected. The timing and count have not been independently verified for this public note. | Use an independently bound actor identity, record the correction and count identical assertions in a prespecified window. Do not infer intent from a model's apology. |
| Responses that felt more or less stable after a reporting period may reflect context or product changes; the cause is unknown. | Compare aligned task sets and independently recorded versions; score factual grounding, identity and execution claims separately. |
| On voice interactions the founder noticed interruptions and cases where background speech appeared to be taken as instructions. | Use synthetic or consented clips; separately record interruptions, recognition errors, and ambient speech treated as an action intent. |

## First-person product pilot, anonymized

The founder served as the first participant and chose an unusually open working process. That amount of openness is not an expectation for other people. At the founder's direction, AI agents researched funding and support opportunities and surfaced options for further checks. Finding options does not prove eligibility, a submitted application, approval, investment or funding in hand. The pilot informed a design goal: help founders spend less time rechecking unsupported answers and move from a concrete doubt to a small, checkable next action.

We are withholding exact details and names pending the applicable permissions and review. Follow this repository: future reports will distinguish hypotheses, evidence, methods and results that others can check. No private transcripts, personal materials or named rankings are published here.
